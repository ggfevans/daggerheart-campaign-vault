# Session 4 - Five Hours to Hell

## 📋 Session Summary
**Duration**: ~4 hours
**Location**: Online  
**Players**: User, Andi
**System Note**: **First session using foundryborne system - excellent UX improvement!**

### Key Events
- **Chasmtender Investigation**: Following lead from Jimmy the bartender about Craven's connection to chasmtenders
- **Void Spire Infiltration**: Party navigates security checkpoint, bureaucracy, and discriminatory treatment
- **Contract Reversal**: From suspects to official contractors after learning of Craven's attacks
- **Descent Begins**: 5-hour elevator journey into Shalk Chasm depths under ooze creature assault

### Critical Information Discovered
- **Craven is attacking chasmtenders**: Not working with them, but systematically destroying their operations
- **The Chainlord**: Ultimate authority overseeing Void Spire operations
- **Official contract secured**: Party hired to stop Craven and recover mysterious artifact
- **Chasm ecosystem hostile**: Ooze creatures and other predators inhabit the depths

### Chasmtenders Political Situation
- **Divided Opinion**: Local community split on chasmtenders' legitimacy
  - **Supporters**: Believe they're doing necessary work keeping Void Spire clean/stable
  - **Critics**: View them as extortionists exploiting community dependence
- **Economic Control**: Life in Anchorpoint entirely based on trade from artifacts pulled from below
- **Monopoly Power**: Chasmtenders control access to Shalk Chasm's valuable resources
- **Shalk Chasm Wealth**: Contains many artifacts including **portal to another world**

### TORQUE'S CRITICAL SIGHTING
- **Craven Visual Confirmation**: Saw **Craven (burly) with 10 men** at the chasm
- **Chasmtender Meeting**: Witnessed them talking to chasmtenders before descent
- **DISAPPEARED**: Craven's entire group **entered chasm and never returned**
- **Timeline**: Sighting suggests recent activity - investigation target may be in chasm
- **Group Size**: Craven traveling with substantial force (10 men)
- **Extortion Evidence**: Chasmtenders wanted "a chestload" from Torque for access

### TORQUE'S EVIDENCE QUEST
- **Needs Proof**: Wants evidence of chasmtender "farce" but **uncertain who to give it to**
- **Authority Confusion**: No clear recipient for evidence - suggests lack of oversight/justice system
- **Wants Documentation**: Seeking **"something solid, some notes"** to prove chasmtender corruption
- **Augustus's Question**: Asked **"for whom?"** regarding proof recipient - highlighting systemic problem
- **Grassroots Opposition**: Individual seeking justice without institutional support
