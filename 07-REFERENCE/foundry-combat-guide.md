# 🗡️ Foundry VTT Combat Quick Guide

*Focused on Combat Mechanics & Common Issues*

## 🚨 **IMMEDIATE HELP: Measurement Tools**

### **Getting Rid of Measurement Lines/Templates**
- **ESC Key**: Clears most active tools and measurements
- **Click Measure Tool Again**: In toolbar (📏) to deactivate
- **Right-Click Empty Space**: Often clears active measurements
- **Delete Key**: While measurement is selected
- **Refresh Page**: F5 - nuclear option, won't lose session progress

### **Measurement Tool Controls**
- **Single Click**: Start measurement
- **Click Again**: Add waypoint/corner
- **Double-Click**: End measurement
- **Right-Click**: Cancel current measurement
- **Ctrl+Z**: Undo last measurement point

## ⚔️ Combat Flow in Foundry

### **Turn Structure (Daggerheart Style)**
1. **No Initiative Order**: Spotlight moves organically
2. **GM Controls Flow**: They'll tell you when it's your turn
3. **Describe First**: Say what you want to do
4. **Then Roll**: GM will ask for rolls when needed
5. **Move Token**: During your turn, drag to new position

### **Your Combat Turn**
1. **Announce Intention**: "I want to attack the goblin"
2. **Move Token**: Drag to where you want to be
3. **Make Rolls**: Use character sheet buttons or chat commands
4. **Apply Results**: GM handles damage/effects
5. **End Turn**: Signal you're done

## 🎯 Targeting & Attacks

### **How to Target Enemies**
1. **Press T**: Activates targeting mode
2. **Click Enemy Token**: Red crosshair appears on target
3. **Use Attack**: Click weapon/spell on character sheet
4. **Auto-Targeting**: Some systems target automatically

### **Clearing Targets**
- **T Key Again**: Deactivates targeting mode
- **Right-Click Empty Space**: Clears all targets
- **Click Target Again**: Removes that specific target

### **Attack Process**
1. **Target Enemy** (if needed)
2. **Click Attack** on character sheet
3. **System Rolls**: Foundry handles the dice
4. **Damage Rolls**: Often automatic after hit
5. **GM Applies**: Damage gets applied to target

## 📏 Movement & Distance

### **Movement Rules**
- **Drag Token**: Move during your turn
- **Ruler Appears**: Shows distance as you move
- **Grid Squares**: Usually 5 feet each
- **Movement Speed**: System tracks your limits

### **Daggerheart Ranges**
- **Melee**: Adjacent squares
- **Very Close**: 5 feet (1 square)
- **Close**: 25 feet (5 squares)
- **Far**: 50 feet (10 squares)
- **Very Far**: 100+ feet (20+ squares)

### **Range Checking**
- **Measure Tool** (📏): Check distances before moving
- **Ruler During Movement**: Automatic distance display
- **Token to Token**: Right-click often shows distance

## 🎲 Combat Rolling

### **Daggerheart Combat Rolls**
- **Attack Roll**: 2d12 + modifier (Hope & Fear dice)
- **Damage Roll**: Usually automatic after successful attack
- **Hope vs Fear**: Higher die determines token generation

### **Using Character Sheet**
- **Weapon Buttons**: Click to roll attack + damage
- **Spell Buttons**: Click for spell attack/effect
- **Ability Buttons**: Click for special actions
- **Manual Rolling**: Use chat `/roll 2d12+3`

### **Reading Roll Results**
- **Total**: Add both d12s + modifier
- **Hope/Fear**: Which die rolled higher
- **Critical**: Both dice show same number
- **Success/Fail**: GM compares to difficulty

## 🛡️ Health & Status

### **Health Tracking**
- **HP Bar**: Usually visible above/below token
- **Character Sheet**: Always shows current HP
- **Auto-Updates**: Damage applied automatically
- **Manual Adjustment**: GM can adjust if needed

### **Status Effects**
- **Right-Click Token**: Configure → Status Effects
- **Common Effects**: Prone, Stunned, Poisoned, etc.
- **Visual Icons**: Appear around token border
- **Duration**: GM tracks timing

### **Daggerheart Stress & Armor**
- **Stress**: Mental/emotional damage track
- **Armor Slots**: Physical damage boxes
- **Recovery**: Different rest mechanics than D&D

## 🎪 Combat Etiquette

### **Do This**
- ✅ **Wait for Your Turn**: Don't move during others' actions
- ✅ **Describe Actions**: "I charge the orc with my sword"
- ✅ **Ask Questions**: "Can I reach that enemy?"
- ✅ **Stay Engaged**: Pay attention to others' turns
- ✅ **Use Token**: Move it to show your position

### **Avoid This**
- ❌ **Moving Others' Tokens**: Only touch your own
- ❌ **Rolling Without Permission**: Wait for GM to call for rolls
- ❌ **Interrupting**: Let current player finish their turn
- ❌ **Arguing Rules**: Save discussions for after combat
- ❌ **Analysis Paralysis**: Make decisions reasonably quickly

## 🚨 Common Combat Issues & Fixes

### **"I Can't Move My Token"**
- **Check Permissions**: GM may need to give you control
- **Combat Lock**: Some systems lock movement during others' turns
- **Right-Click Token**: Check if you own it (should show green border)
- **Ask GM**: They can adjust permissions

### **"My Attacks Aren't Working"**
- **Check Targeting**: Make sure enemy is targeted
- **Character Sheet**: Use buttons instead of manual rolling
- **Permissions**: GM may need to enable attack permissions
- **Refresh**: F5 if character sheet isn't responding

### **"I Can't See My Rolls"**
- **Chat Tab**: Make sure you're looking at chat sidebar
- **Scroll Down**: Chat may have scrolled up
- **Whisper vs Public**: Check if you rolled privately by accident
- **System Issue**: Ask if others can see your rolls

### **"Measurements Are Stuck"**
- **ESC Key**: Universal "cancel" command
- **Click Tool Again**: Deactivate measure tool in toolbar
- **Refresh Page**: F5 - won't lose your progress
- **Ask GM**: They can clear stuck measurements

### **"Combat Is Too Confusing"**
- **Focus on Description**: Say what you want to do
- **Let System Handle Math**: Click buttons instead of manual calculation
- **Ask for Help**: Other players and GM are there to help
- **One Thing at a Time**: Move, then attack, then end turn

## 🎯 Quick Combat Checklist

### **Start of Combat**

- [ ] Can you see the enemy tokens?

- [ ] Target enemy (if required)
- [ ] Make attack/action using character sheet
- [ ] Confirm results with GM
- [ ] Signal you're done

### **End of Combat**
- [ ] Clear any active targeting
- [ ] Check your HP/status
- [ ] Update any resources used
- [ ] Ask questions about what happened

## 📞 Emergency Combat Help

### **If You're Completely Lost**
1. **Say "I'm stuck"**: In Discord voice chat
2. **Describe Problem**: "I can't move" or "My sheet won't work"
3. **Share Screen**: GM can see what you're seeing
4. **Let GM Help**: They can control your token temporarily
5. **Learn for Next Time**: Ask for explanation after combat

### **Quick Fixes**
- **F5**: Refresh browser (won't lose progress)
- **ESC**: Cancel whatever tool is active
- **Right-Click**: Opens context menus for most things
- **Double-Click**: Usually opens character sheets
- **Ask GM**: Geoff is noted as very new-player friendly
