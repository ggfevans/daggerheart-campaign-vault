# Battlewagon Upgrade Priority List

## Prerequisites
⚠️ **ONLY PROCEED** after completing [[battlewagon-high-mileage-priorities|High Mileage Maintenance Priorities]]

This list assumes your 2011 Jetta SEL has passed mechanical assessment and essential maintenance is complete.

## Tier 1: Core Capability Upgrades

### 1. Falken Wildpeak A/T Trail Tires (235/45R17) - $520-620 🎯 **FOUNDATION**
**Why First:** Tires are your only contact with terrain - everything else is secondary
**PNW Specific:**
- Three-Peak Mountain Snowflake certification
- Superior wet weather performance (your #1 need)
- Proven on Fraser Valley logging roads
- Quiet highway manners for daily driving

**Size Rationale:**
- 235/45R17 = max size for lifted Jettas without rubbing
- Maintains speedo accuracy
- More sidewall protection than stock 225/45R17

**Installation:** Easy DIY with tire machine access

### 2. Forge Motorsport 1.5" Lift Kit - $400-500 🎯 **CLEARANCE**
**Why This Kit:**
- Bolt-on installation (no strut disassembly)
- Maintains suspension geometry
- 1.5" = sweet spot for capability without problems
- Real-world tested on VW platforms

**Fraser Valley Relevance:**
- Ground clearance for Harrison Lake FSRs
- Chilliwack River valley logging roads
- Approach angle improvement for creek crossings

**Installation:** 4-6 hours DIY, moderate difficulty

### 3. DieselGeek Panzer Plate Skid Kit - $400-500 🛡️ **PROTECTION**
**Components:**
- Engine oil pan protection
- Transmission skid plate
- 0.190" aluminum construction
- Stainless steel mounting hardware

**Why Essential:**
- Your oil pan sits dangerously low
- One rock = $2000+ engine replacement
- 5-minute removal for maintenance access
- 22lb weight penalty acceptable

## Tier 2: Enhanced Capability

### 4. Bilstein B6 Heavy Duty Struts - $400-600 🏔️ **HANDLING**
**Why Upgrade:**
- Factory struts worn at 213k km anyway
- Lift kit changes suspension dynamics
- Heavy-duty units handle loaded weight better
- Essential for safe mountain driving

**Fraser Valley Specific:**
- Coquihalla Highway steep grades
- Loaded camping gear stability
- Improved braking control on descents

### 5. External Transmission Cooler - $100-200 🌡️ **LONGEVITY**
**Already covered in maintenance, but critical for:**
- Mountain pass driving (Sea-to-Sky, Coquihalla)
- Towing small trailers
- Extended FSR driving in summer heat

### 6. Thule Evo Roof Rack System - $350-400 📦 **UTILITY**
**Specifications:**
- 165lb dynamic load rating
- Low wind noise design
- No drilling installation
- Compatible with various accessories

**Fraser Valley Use Cases:**
- Camping gear for multi-day trips
- Kayak transport (Harrison Lake, Cultus Lake)
- Recovery boards and extra fuel storage

## Installation Strategy

### DIY vs Professional
**Easy DIY (1-2 hours):**
- Tire installation (with machine access)
- Floor liners
- Recovery equipment mounting

**Moderate DIY (Half day):**
- Lift kit installation
- LED light bar wiring
- Transmission cooler

**Consider Professional:**
- Skid plate installation (access challenges)
- Roof rack if uncomfortable with torque specs
- Strut replacement (spring compressor needed)

### Tool Requirements
**Essential Tools:**
- Floor jack and jack stands
- Basic socket set (metric)
- Torque wrench
- Wire strippers/crimpers
- Multi-meter for electrical work

**Specialty Tools (borrow/rent):**
- Spring compressor (strut work)
- Transmission fluid pump
- Brake bleeding kit

## Fraser Valley Testing Locations

### Initial Testing (Post-Installation)
- **Hope Nickel Plate Road** - Easy FSR for initial testing
- **Harrison Lake West FSR** - Moderate difficulty, good surface
- **Vedder Mountain** - Steeper grades, traction testing

### Progressive Difficulty
- **Chilliwack River FSRs** - More challenging terrain
- **Silver Lake Road** - Technical sections
- **Cheakamus FSRs** - Advanced testing
