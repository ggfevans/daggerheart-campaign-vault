# High Mileage Maintenance Priorities (213k km)

## Critical Understanding
At 213k km (132k miles), your 2011 Jetta SEL is in the **danger zone** for several expensive failures. These aren't optional upgrades - they're overdue maintenance preventing catastrophic breakdowns.

## Immediate Priority Items

### 1. Vacuum Pump Delete Kit - $99-149 ⚡ **CRITICAL**
**Timeline:** IMMEDIATE - before any adventure driving
**Why Critical:** 
- Vacuum pump failure typically occurs 80k-100k miles (you're at 132k)
- Failure causes loss of brake assist - dangerous on mountain passes
- Oil leaks damage other components
- $1000+ repair vs $150 prevention

**Parts Sources:**
- Spulen Ultimate Brake Vacuum Pump Delete Kit
- IE (Integrated Engineering) alternative
- USP Motorsports option

**Installation:** 3-4 hours DIY, moderate difficulty

### 2. Complete Ignition System Overhaul - $200-250 ⚡ **URGENT**
**Timeline:** Within 1000 km
**Components:**
- All 5 red-top coil packs (06E905115G)
- NGK spark plugs (specific to 2.5L)
- Inspect for carbon buildup

**Why Critical:**
- Factory coils fail 60-80k miles (you're 50k+ overdue)
- Misfires cause engine damage at high mileage
- Poor performance in mountain driving

### 3. PCV System Replacement - $150-300 ⚡ **ESSENTIAL**
**Timeline:** Next 2000 km
**Components:**
- PCV valve
- Associated vacuum lines
- Inspect intake manifold for oil contamination

**Why Critical:**
- Common 2.5L failure causing oil consumption
- Rough idle, poor performance
- Accelerates engine wear if ignored

### 4. Transmission Service + Cooler - $250-350 ⚡ **PREVENT FAILURE**
**Timeline:** ASAP - 09G failures common at 240k km
**Service Required:**
- Complete fluid change (NOT flush)
- VW-spec ATF only (G055025A2)
- External cooler installation
- Filter replacement if accessible

**Why Critical:**
- 09G transmissions fail around 150k miles without service
- Mountain driving accelerates wear
- $4000+ replacement vs $350 prevention

### 5. Electrical System Audit - $200-400 ⚡ **SAFETY**
**Timeline:** Next service interval
**Triggered by:** Wiper motor failure indicates broader issues

**Check:**
- All window regulators (common VW failure)
- Door handle actuators
- Central locking system
- Ground connections (corrosion prone)
- Battery and charging system

## Age-Related Preventive Items

- Water pump inspection

**Why Important:**

- Guide rail wear
- Upper timing cover inspection

**Why Important:**

- 2.5L engines known for chain stretch
- Inspection prevents catastrophic failure

### 8. Brake System Overhaul - $400-600
**Fraser Valley Specific:**
- Brake line corrosion check (coastal climate)
- Fluid replacement (mountain driving demands)
- Pad/rotor assessment
- Vacuum system integrity

## Assessment Strategy

### Pre-Work Inspection
Before spending money on maintenance:
1. **Compression test** - reveals engine condition
2. **Leak-down test** - identifies internal wear
3. **Transmission shift quality** - assess before service investment

### Go/No-Go Thresholds
**PROCEED with maintenance if:**
- Compression >140 psi all cylinders
- Variation <10% between cylinders
- No major oil leaks
- Transmission shifts smoothly when warm

**CONSIDER ABANDONING if:**
- Compression <120 psi any cylinder
- Major variation between cylinders
- Significant oil consumption (>1L/1000km)
- Transmission slipping or harsh shifts
