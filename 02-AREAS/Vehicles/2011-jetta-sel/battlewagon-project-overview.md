# 2011 Jetta SEL Battlewagon Project Overview

## Project Summary
Transform 2011 Volkswagen Jetta SEL (213k km) into a reliable Pacific Northwest battlewagon capable of forest service roads and all-weather driving.

**Budget:** Under $5,000 CAD
**Timeline:** Phased approach based on mechanical condition
**Location:** Fraser Valley, BC (Yale/Hope/Chilliwack area)
**Current Status:** Assessment phase - addressing immediate maintenance needs

## Key Specifications
- **Engine:** 2.5L 5-cylinder (CBUA/CBTA)
- **Transmission:** 09G 6-speed automatic
- **Mileage:** 213k km (132k miles)
- **Age:** 13+ years in coastal BC climate

## Project Goals
1. **Reliability:** Address high-mileage failure points before modifications
2. **Forest Service Road Capability:** Light ground clearance and protection
3. **All-Weather Performance:** Optimize for PNW wet conditions
4. **Daily Drivability:** Maintain comfort and efficiency

## Decision Matrix
### Go/No-Go Criteria
**PROCEED if:**
- Rust limited to surface/cosmetic areas
- Subframe and suspension mounts structurally sound
- Engine compression within acceptable range
- Transmission responds well to service

**ABANDON if:**
- Structural rust in critical areas
- Subframe corrosion
- Major engine/transmission wear
- Repair costs exceed vehicle value
