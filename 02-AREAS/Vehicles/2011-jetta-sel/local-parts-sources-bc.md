## Fluids & Consumables

### VW-Specific Requirements
**Engine Oil:**
- **Spec:** VW 502.00 or 504.00
- **Local sources:** Canadian Tire (Castrol GTX), NAPA
- **Online:** Liqui Moly from Amazon.ca

**Transmission Fluid:**
- **Spec:** VW G055025A2 only
- **Source:** VW dealers (most reliable)
- **Alternative:** FCP Euro for genuine VW fluid

**Coolant:**
- **Spec:** VW G12++ (purple)
- **Local:** VW dealers, some NAPA stores
- **Note:** Do NOT mix coolant types

## Cost Comparison Strategy

### Price Shopping Approach
1. **Get part numbers** from dealer/online
2. **Compare local prices** (NAPA, Parts Source)
3. **Check online pricing** including shipping/duty
4. **Factor in timing** - emergency vs planned

### Budget-Friendly Strategies
**Mix and match sources:**
- **Critical parts:** FCP Euro (lifetime warranty)
- **Wear items:** Local competitive pricing
- **Tools:** Princess Auto sales
- **Fluids:** Canadian Tire bulk purchases
