# Void Spire

## 🗺️ Location Overview
**Type**: Supernatural Landmark / Massive Magical Structure  
**Region**: Shalk Chasm Ridge  
**Status**: **Teetering precariously** on chasm edge  
**First Seen**: Session 3 - Arrival at Anchorpoint  
**Approached**: Session 4 - Direct investigation  

## 📖 Description
An **enormous hulking spire** made of supernatural flame, teetering dangerously on the edge of the **Shalk Chasm** ridge. The structure appears to be barely contained, with **hundreds of chains** stretched across its surface in a desperate attempt to secure it to the cliff face.

### Physical Characteristics
- **Size**: Massive - visible from great distances
- **Material**: Supernatural flame/void energy
- **Stability**: **Precarious** - teetering on chasm edge
- **Restraints**: Hundreds of chains attempting to anchor it
- **Location**: Edge of Shalk Chasm ridge

### Magical Properties
- **Supernatural Flame**: Not ordinary fire - void/arcane energy
- **Instability**: Constantly threatening to fall into the chasm
- **Chain Restraints**: Massive engineering effort to contain it
